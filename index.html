About Us

CaFe is a coffee shop in Herat, Afghanistan, built around a simple idea: coffee brings people together.

Our vision is a welcoming place for everyday pauses, conversations with friends, and the pleasure of a cup of coffee. This website introduces that vision while our menu and visitor details are being finalized.

What Matters to Us

  • Hospitality that makes people feel welcome.
  • An appreciation for coffee and the moments shared over it.
  • A connection to the community in Herat.
